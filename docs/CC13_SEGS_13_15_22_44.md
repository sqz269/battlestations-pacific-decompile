## Uncertainties

- 0078D6C0 samples the water at the first two components of each point and compares the
  height with the second component. That is what the listing does; whether the caller's
  vectors are ordered so that this is (x, z) was not traced.
- 00535B20's message layout and the meaning of the three globals 00F87574..7C were not traced
  to their writers; the name is a hypothesis.
- 0051B250 / 0051B8C0 ignore their two stack arguments (RET 8), as the image does.
- Checked-iterator tests that compare a container's own address with null or with itself
  cannot fail and are omitted where noted in the code; every index or range test is kept.
- Win32 `EnterCriticalSection`, `LeaveCriticalSection`, `DeleteCriticalSection` and
  `InterlockedDecrement` are called directly on the image's own objects.
