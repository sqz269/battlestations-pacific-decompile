* Font+4C is first zeroed as eight DWORDs; its +12 WORD becomes CURRENT signed Font+14 divided by four with truncation toward zero; +18/+1C become GFX/alpha (absolute +64/+68). These two slots hold the Font's owning returned references.

The remaining long-lived local name header is finally returned through current pool getter/return services. Constructor-unwritten Font+00, +16/+17 and +49..4B are not blanket-zeroed by this load.
