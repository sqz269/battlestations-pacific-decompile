## Failure and floating-point boundaries

Lookup failure occurs under17. During active23 or24, ordinary guard unwinding
must lower17 and destroy only actual S6C, then let the enclosing Msh/iterator
owners handle propagation. Once Chance lowers17, a failure at its final raw
store has the Native metadata's17 chain, whereas Threshold's final store still
