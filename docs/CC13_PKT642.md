# cc13-pkt642: the HUD unit-marker builder and its two widget writers

Packet cc13-pkt642 (2026-10-16): 00642040 with its callees 0063C620 and 0063D1E0 (segment 26), taken
together as docs/CC13_SWEEPB6.md "Deferred" asked. Names are hypotheses, not recovered symbols.
Files: `include/bsp/cc13_pkt642.hpp`, `src/cc13_pkt642.cpp` (registered on bsp_core in
`cmake/startup.cmake`), this document. Nothing is wired into the game and no host file was edited.

The host interface `Cc13Pkt642Host` extends sweepB6's `Cc13SweepB6Host`, so the sweepB..B6 hooks are
reused as they are. Two earlier reconstructions are called directly:
`cc13_sweepB2_hud_marker_project_offscreen_arrow_00638c60` and
`cc13_sweepB2_hud_markers_update_lead_marker_00640a20`. New hooks: 00638B90, 00639810, 0059CB90,
00639990, 006407A0, 004323D0 (STL push_back), 005220C0, 00803DC0, 0063E5C0 and 0077A2F0.

Status of every function here: reconstructed, build-tested; not ABI-compatible, not fixture- or game-validated.
No draws from the shared RNG 00BD2F10 (none of these functions calls it directly).

## Method

Each body was read whole from the listing with ESP tracked after every push, so every by-value
aggregate and every `LEA reg,[ESP+n]` argument was mapped to its frame slot. Constants were read from
the PE (`tools/pe_const_read.py` plus their bit patterns) and are kept with their image width (float
vs double). x87 register-register operations in 0063D1E0 were read from the bytes, not the listing:
`DE E1` FSUBRP ST1 (ST0 - ST1), `DE E9` FSUBP ST1 (ST1 - ST0), `DE C1` FADDP. Float compares follow
the image's jumps: JBE is "<= or unordered", JC "< or unordered", JNC ">= and ordered",
UCOMISS/LAHF/TEST AH,44h/JP is "not equal or unordered".

## 00640D70 BSP_InGameHudMarkers_UpdateTargetSectionCallout (sweepB6 list, segment 26)

`__thiscall(ECX = markers screen)`, RET; an EBP frame aligned to 8. It is the third caller of
0063C620 and calls this pass's reconstruction directly. Reg-reg x87 forms were read from the bytes
(DC C9 / D8 C9 / DE C9 for the half-size and centre products, DE E2 for `0.5 - x`, DC E0 for the

- Returns unless the +20h target exists, is kind 6 and its parts object (0080E490) +14h is not 1.
  Reads the eye point from the camera [[00E188A8]+19FCh] (+120h..+128h, after 00B6DB70 unless bit 1
  of +5Ch is set). Leaves through 0063BED0 when there is no controlled vehicle, the target is on the
  local party's team, or 00804620 (the controlled vehicle's sensors, kind 5) misses the target.
- Pass 1 walks the target's section vector (visual class 00476B90 + 3Ch, 8-byte elements) with the
  inlined checked iterator (00BF6713 on each failed check), and each element's part vector (2Ch-byte
  parts, 00637660). Parts of type 8, 5 or 6 are pushed (006403F0); a part whose projected box (the
  first seven of its eight corners) contains the screen centre competes on the distance from its box
  centre to the eye (00419440), nearest wins.
- When pass 1 found nothing, pass 2 projects each pushed part's eight corners and centre and keeps
  the part with a projected point nearest the screen centre within 0.04f.
- The chosen part's type selects the key (8 `ingame.sections_magazine`, 5 `ingame.sections_engine`,

Two image details are kept as they are: only seven of the eight corners feed the screen bounds (both

call is the last transformed corner's world x/y, which sits right after the corners in the frame.

## 005F0CD0 BSP_OptionsScreen_RefreshHelpLine (sweepB6 list, segment 23)

- Rebuild 2: accept (command A2h, placement 0), default (A7h), navigate (B3h), change (AFh), cancel

- Rebuild 3 by the mode [00E19650]: 7 gives accept, an empty label (command 0), navigate, change,

The mode-4 command is the dword at the frame slot that held the cancel string's length, with only its
low byte rewritten; the C++ keeps that dword (its upper bytes are zero for that length).
