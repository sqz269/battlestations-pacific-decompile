# CC13 VER 14: USN21 strike losses are not fighter kills (cc13-ver14)

Packet cc13-ver14 was a read-only verification for cc9. The question was why so many strike losses go to fighters
("killer_cat 1", about 45 of 126 USN21 dive deaths in `docs/CC13_VER_9.md` section 2). The plan was to check
four fighter terms against the image: CAP intercept, dogfight, escort follow and the tail gunner.

Method: existing logs and this installation's mission files only. There were no runs, no Ghidra writes and no
C++ changes.

## Finding: killer_cat 1 is the ship AA machine gun

- **What `killer_cat` records.** It is the gun category of the last damaging hit.
  - `dt.last_category = gun.category` (`src/game_hosts_gunnery.cpp:10308`) is printed by the `death row` note
    (`:11737`).
  - Category 1 is `AAMACHINEGUN` (`src/gunnery_tables.cpp:17`). Plane guns are category 0, `PLANEGUN`.
- **What the log shows.** `J:\PROG\battlestations-pacific-decompile-cc9-ships44\local\s46_e_u21d.log` has
  197 `death row` lines.
  - By category: 56 cat 1, 9 cat 5, 127 cat 6, 2 cat 7 and 3 with no killer.
  - Every cat-1 row names a Japanese ship as `killer`, for example Naganami, Myoko, Okinami, Kishinami,
    Yahagi, Haguro, Kongo or Yamato.
  - `hits c0` (plane-gun hits) is 0 on all 197 rows.
  - No Japanese squadron appears in the log. Only the `Intrepid/Essex/Cabot/Lexington/Enterprise/Franklin_sqnNN`
    names occur, and no Japanese aircraft dies.
- **Two bands of cat-1 kills, by altitude and killer range.**
  - Low: altitude 114-375 m at 160-640 m, in the aimglide or pull-out.
  - High: altitude 850-1250 m at 1000-1404 m, on the flyabove leg.
  - Japanese 25 mm round class 46 has V0 800 and range 1600 in this run's own `gunnery: bullet class` lines, so
    every high-band kill is inside the authored range.

## The image has no Japanese fighters here either

`scripts/missions/usn/usn_sibuyan.lua` (2024-10-29) and `universe/scenes/missions/usn/usn_sibuyan.scn` hold
these `PlaneSquadronGen` entities:

- `F6F_1`..`F6F_3`, which are US (`Mission.Hellcats`, `:917-919`);
- `JakeTmpl`, one floatplane generated by `luaJakeTime` (`:725`);
- `Kamikaze1`..`Kamikaze15`, of type `KamikazeZero`.
  - `luaMoveToPh4` generates these only in phase 4 (`:884-899`), after the strike phases.
  - The objective text is "Destroy the kamikaze wave!".

So in USN21 the fighter CAP, the dogfight, the escort follow and the bomber tail gunner add nothing to strike
losses, in the image or in the host. Auditing them cannot move USN21 release counts.

## Where the losses do come from

These deaths are ship AA. The cat-1 chain was already compared with the image and found faithful by cc13-mgaa1
(`docs/CC13_AUDIT_MG_AA_LETHALITY.md` section 2). That covers the cadence, the throw cone, the aim error, the mesh
hit test, the per-hit damage and the kill rule.

The target-selection terms are bound in `docs/AA_TARGETING.md` and its corrections:

- the category range origin;
- the minimum range of kinds 5 and 6;
- the AAGunnerBot armour test;
- the fire window;
- the leader penalty, `kAaLeaderPenaltyBound` (`00863A4F`).

The open AA items are unchanged. They are:

- mgaa1 open item 1, the AA-bot tick rate against the host's 0.05 s gunnery tick;
- the gun node frame for the fire window;
- the line-of-fire installer behind `0072F6E0`.

The levers for USN21 strike survival are those in `docs/CC13_VER_9.md`: how long the strike spends inside the
envelope (the 1450 m attackrun and the ctl+394h cap). They are not fighter AI.
