## Bounded raw Text domain and general frontier

The existing91-byte provider is usable directly when all these contracts actually hold:

- SAME successful raw Text identity/prefix from the genuine base/default producer, with live+04; no semantic `GuiWidgetOwner` cast or newly initialized counter.
- A real independent creator and actual Text storage are held alive until ALL material-owned Text references in this bounded operation are released. Other lifetime actors cannot directly destroy/return that Text slot during the borrow.
- SAME actual material/resource registry and services remain valid for any reached old-owner terminal. Old owner survives through its current release; incoming Text survives independently, including equal-owner replacement.
- Full material/factory/caller acquisition graph remains retained before effects and through failure. No alias is promoted into ownership or used for discovery after an object lifetime ends.

A held count/creator is **not** a scalar-delete or ancestor barrier: `AB8EE0` calls destruction without decrementing or checking+04, and retaining Text does not retain its page/ClipBox/ancestor.
